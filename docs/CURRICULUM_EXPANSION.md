# CHEM-SIM: Full Degree Curriculum, Teaching Studio & Career-Readiness Expansion

## 1. Product Vision & Architecture

CHEM-SIM bridges the gap between academic theory, practical laboratory experimentation, plant design, and industrial employability.
Rather than locking into a rigid university syllabus that becomes obsolete when course codes or semesters shuffle, CHEM-SIM organizes engineering around permanent **core chemical engineering competencies** mapped flexibly across Semesters III through VIII:

```
Academic Learning (Sem III - VIII)
      │
      ▼
Student Understanding Engine ───► Faculty Teaching Studio
      │                                     │
      ▼                                     ▼
Virtual Labs & Digital Twins ◄─── Process Design Studio
      │
      ▼
Career & Placement Readiness Centre (10 Tracks & Plant Troubleshooting)
```

## 2. Complete Curriculum Integration (Semesters III to VIII)

Every subject follows an **8-Stage Structured Learning Pathway**:
1. **Concept Introduction**: Qualitative overview and physical motivation.
2. **Visual Explanation**: Dynamic animated diagram or profile.
3. **Mathematical Derivation**: First-principles continuum mechanics or thermodynamics derivation.
4. **Worked Analytical Example**: Step-by-step numerical calculation with intermediate units.
5. **Interactive Simulation**: Live slider-driven experiment or virtual apparatus.
6. **Engineering Application**: Real-world plant scale or industrial case study.
7. **Assessment & Practice**: Problem sets and oral viva examination.
8. **Industry Connection**: Software tools used in EPCs, licensors, and plant sites.

### Semester III: Chemical Engineering Foundations
- **CHE31: Integral Transforms, Complex Analysis and PDE** (Calculus, Fourier series, Laplace transforms, Navier-Stokes partial differential equations).
- **CHE32: Momentum Transfer** (Fluid statics, laminar vs. turbulent flow, Navier-Stokes, Darcy-Weisbach friction factor, boundary layer theory, centrifugal pump hydraulics).
- **CHE33: Chemical Process Calculations** (Units and dimensions, material balances with recycle/bypass/purge, combustion stoichiometry, energy balances, psychrometry).
- **CHE34: Mechanical Operations** (Particle size analysis, crushing & grinding laws, settling & sedimentation, filtration, centrifugation, fluidisation and packed bed dynamics).
- **CHE35: Technical Chemistry** (Physical chemistry, catalysis, chemical kinetics, organic intermediates).
- **Virtual Labs**: Fluid Flow Equipment Lab & Mechanical Operations Lab.

### Semester IV: Core Engineering Principles
- **CHE41: Numerical Methods and Statistics** (Root finding, numerical integration, Runge-Kutta for ODEs, finite difference for PDEs, regression analysis).
- **CHE42: Heat Transfer** (Fourier conduction, film convection, Dittus-Boelter, nucleate boiling, condensation, LMTD and $\epsilon$-NTU exchanger design).
- **CHE43: Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics** (First and Second Laws, Equations of State, Residual properties, VLE, Margules/Wilson/NRTL, chemical reaction equilibrium).
- **CHE44: Materials Science & Corrosion** (Crystal structures, phase diagrams, iron-carbon equilibrium, corrosion mechanisms, materials selection for severe chemical services).
- **CHE45: Environmental Engineering** (Air pollution dispersion, industrial wastewater treatment, solid waste management, environmental regulations).
- **Virtual Labs**: Heat Transfer Lab & Technical Chemistry/Thermodynamics Lab.

### Semester V: Core Chemical Engineering & Elective I
- **CHE51: Mass Transfer I** (Fickian molecular diffusion, film theory, boundary layer mass transfer, gas absorption, packed column hydraulics, NTU-HTU calculations).
- **CHE52: Chemical Reaction Engineering I** (Homogeneous reaction kinetics, ideal reactor design: batch, CSTR, PFR; series and parallel reactions, temperature and pressure effects).
- **CHE53: Process Instrumentation and Dynamics** (Sensor technologies, first and second order dynamic systems, feedback control loops, P/PI/PID controllers).
- **CHE54: Process Utilities and Safety** (Steam generation and distribution, cooling towers, compressed air, industrial water treatment, plant electrical safety).
- **Configurable Elective I (CHE55X)**:
  - *CHE551: Petroleum Refining Engineering* (Crude assay, atmospheric and vacuum distillation, catalytic cracking, hydroprocessing).
  - *CHE552: Material Science & Engineering* (Advanced polymers, ceramics, composites, electronic materials).
  - *CHE553: Non-Conventional Energy Sources* (Solar thermal, photovoltaics, wind energy, biomass conversion, hydrogen fuel cells).
  - *CHE554: Interfacial Science & Colloidal Engineering* (Surface tension, Young-Laplace, surfactants, micellization, emulsion stability).
  - *CHE555: Polymer Processing and Technology* (Polymerization kinetics, rheology, extrusion, injection molding).
- **Virtual Labs**: Momentum & Reaction Lab & Process Control Lab.

### Semester VI: Advanced Separation & Elective II
- **CHE61: Mass Transfer II** (Multicomponent distillation, liquid-liquid extraction, leaching, membrane separations, adsorption, drying).
- **CHE62: Chemical Reaction Engineering II** (Non-ideal flow, residence time distribution (RTD), heterogeneous catalysis, Langmuir-Hinshelwood, Thiele modulus).
- **CHE63: Transport Phenomena** (Coupled shell momentum, energy, and mass balances; equations of change; turbulent transport analogies: Reynolds, Colburn, Chilton-Colburn).
- **CHE64: Industrial Pollution Control** (Particulate scrubbers, flue gas desulfurization, biological wastewater treatment, advanced oxidation processes).
- **Configurable Elective II (CHE64X)**:
  - *CHE641: Process Safety, HAZOP and Risk Analysis* (HAZOP methodology, fault tree analysis, SIL/SIS allocation, dispersion modeling).
  - *CHE642: Operations Research and Optimization* (Linear programming, simplex method, nonlinear optimization, supply chain logistics).
  - *CHE643: Industrial Wastewater Treatment* (Activated sludge process, anaerobic digestion, membrane bioreactors).
  - *CHE644: Process Modeling and Simulation* (Dynamic modeling, equation-oriented simulators, sequential modular flowsheeting).
  - *CHE645: Biochemical Engineering* (Enzyme kinetics, Michaelis-Menten, bioreactor design, sterile processing).
- **Virtual Labs**: Mass Transfer Operations Lab & Reaction Engineering Simulation Lab.

### Semester VII: Plant Design, Economics & Elective III
- **CHE71: Chemical Equipment Design and Drawing** (Design of shell and tube heat exchangers, distillation columns, pressure vessels per ASME Sec VIII Div 1).
- **CHE72: Plant Design and Process Economics** (Process synthesis, capital cost estimation, operating cost, profitability metrics: NPV, IRR, payback period).
- **CHE73: Safety, Health and Loss Prevention** (Toxicity thresholds, flash points, runaway reactions, pressure relief sizing, OSHA/COMAH compliance).
- **Configurable Elective III (CHE73X)**:
  - *CHE731: Drug Delivery Systems & Pharmaceutical Engineering* (Controlled drug release, encapsulation, sterile processing, cGMP guidelines).
  - *CHE732: Natural Gas Engineering and Processing* (Reservoir fluids, sweetening, dehydration, NGL fractionation, LNG liquefaction).
  - *CHE733: Advanced Separation Technologies* (Supercritical fluid extraction, simulated moving bed adsorption, pervaporation).
  - *CHE734: Pinch Analysis and Heat Integration* (Composite curves, grid diagrams, minimum utility targeting, heat exchanger network synthesis).
  - *CHE735: Solid Waste Management and Resource Recovery* (Waste characterization, thermal incineration, pyrolysis, circular economy).
- **Virtual Labs**: Computer Aided Process Design (CAD/CAM) Lab & Simulation Studio.

### Semester VIII: Capstone & Industrial Transition
- **CHE81: Major Engineering Capstone Project** (Feasibility study, flowsheet synthesis, equipment sizing, HAZOP analysis, economic evaluation).
- **CHE82: Industrial Internship and In-Plant Training** (8-16 weeks industrial placement, shift logs, technical report, viva).
- **CHE83: Professional Ethics, Management and Entrepreneurship** (Engineering ethics, contract management, patent law, techno-entrepreneurship).
- **CHE84: Technical Seminar and Research Paper** (Literature review, critical technical synthesis, scientific presentation).

## 3. Faculty Teaching Studio

Built specifically for chemical engineering professors to run interactive, visually compelling lectures without switching tools:

1. **Interactive Whiteboard**:
   - Drag-and-drop process equipment symbols (Centrifugal Pump, Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger, CSTR, Distillation Column, Storage Vessels).
   - Dynamic connection streams with mass flow, temperature, and pressure tags.
   - Live chalkboard annotation overlay for writing equations directly beside equipment.

2. **Live Simulation Presentation Stage**:
   - Fullscreen distraction-free auditorium mode.
   - Dynamic laser pointer with coordinate tracking for calling out internal components (impeller vanes, volute cutwater, double mechanical seal).
   - Side-by-side comparative simulation: Scenario A (Direct discharge valve throttling) vs Scenario B (VFD speed regulation at 2400 RPM) with direct efficiency and electrical energy savings readout.
   - Pause / Resume freeze-frame capability and beginner mode toggle.

3. **Step-by-Step Derivation Mode**:
   - 9 rigorous continuum mechanics and unit operations derivations.
   - Interactive step-by-step reveal buttons that allow faculty to walk through assumptions, control volumes, differentials, boundary conditions, and final engineering forms.

4. **Classroom Interaction & Polls**:
   - Live numerical polls and concept check questions (e.g. "What happens to pump shut-off head when fluid density is doubled?").
   - Instant response distribution graph and automated pedagogical explanations.

5. **Student Lab Submission Grading**:
   - Review virtual laboratory observation tables, $H-Q$ curves, and viva responses.
   - Apply grading rubrics with direct feedback.

## 4. Student Understanding Engine

Empowers students to develop deep physical intuition before crunching numbers:

1. **Interactive Physics Diagrams**:
   - Dynamic SVG diagrams for velocity profiles, LMTD profiles, reactor conversions, vapor-liquid equilibrium, and phase diagrams.
2. **Equation Explorer**:
   - Clickable interactive terms in Darcy-Weisbach, Ergun, LMTD, CSTR design, Arrhenius, and Fick's laws.
   - Displays physical meaning, SI units, dimensions, sensitivity analysis, and industrial examples.
3. **8-Step Universal Problem Solver**:
   - Structured methodology: Knowns $\to$ Unknowns $\to$ Governing Equation $\to$ Unit Dimensional Check $\to$ Numerical Substitution $\to$ Calculation $\to$ Physical Interpretation $\to$ Sanity Check.
4. **Misconception Detector**:
   - Interactive diagnostic tests targeting common chemical engineering misconceptions:
     - Pump Head vs. Fluid Density
     - Minimum Fluidisation Pressure Drop Plateau
     - Counter-Current Exchanger Temperature Cross
     - Chemical Equilibrium vs. Reaction Rate
     - Reflux Ratio vs. Reboiler Duty
     - Pipe Diameter Halving vs. Pressure Drop ($2^5 = 32\times$)
     - Thiele Modulus and Internal Diffusion Limitations
     - Ideal Gas Law at High Pressures
5. **Degree Knowledge Graph**:
   - Interactive visual network illustrating how fundamental physics (Calculus, Physics, Fluid Mechanics) flows into unit operations, reactor design, and industrial plant troubleshooting.
